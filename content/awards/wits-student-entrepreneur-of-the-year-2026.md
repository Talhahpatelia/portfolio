# Student Entrepreneur of the Year, Wits Student Leadership Awards

The Student Leadership Awards are Wits's awards for what students do outside the lecture hall: co-curricular and extra-curricular activity, entrepreneurial and innovative thinking, and service to the Wits community and beyond. Student Entrepreneur of the Year is one of the categories.

I nominated myself in that category in 2026. Nominations closed on 30 September and needed a CV, a motivation letter and testimonial letters. I also sent a portfolio of evidence covering GotchaEducation, NavigoTransport and the Wits HPC results.

I won the category at the ceremony on 23 October 2026. Jared Swart, who coached the AFRETEC SEED team with me, received a Student Leadership Award at the same ceremony.
