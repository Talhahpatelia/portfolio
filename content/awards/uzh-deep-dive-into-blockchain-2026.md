# First place in four challenges, Deep Dive into Blockchain

Deep Dive into Blockchain (DDiB) is a summer school run by the University of Zurich Blockchain Center. Its 7th edition ran from 5 to 24 July 2026 at Stellenbosch University. I took part on an industry scholarship that covered the CHF 3,500 fee.

I placed first in four parts of the programme:

- the final project, which won the best group project medal;
- the Proof-of-Work mining challenge;
- the Cardano staking challenge;
- the DAO governance and voting challenge.

## The final project

With four teammates I built Pacy, a Cardano-based digital prescription system. A pharmacy can check that a prescription is genuine, a prescription cannot be used again once it has been dispensed, and the medical data is encrypted and kept off the blockchain. The Cardano Academy supported the project.
