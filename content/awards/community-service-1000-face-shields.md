# 3D-printed face shields for medical staff

During the first South African lockdown in 2020 I 3D printed face shields for medical staff. The Salaam Foundation distributed them to medical professionals.

Salaamedia's post of 7 April 2020 reported that 530 shields had been delivered, with more than 1,000 expected. By 29 April 2020 the Community Hours group was reporting that I had made more than 1,000. Salaamedia also interviewed me about the project during the lockdown.

Reddam House Bedfordview gave me Full Colours for community service at the end of 2020, for more than 200 hours.
