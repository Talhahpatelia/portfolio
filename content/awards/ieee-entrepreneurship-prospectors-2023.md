# Winning pitch, IEEE Entrepreneurship Prospectors workshop

The IEEE Entrepreneurship Prospectors workshop ran at the Wits Innovation Centre from 10 to 14 July 2023, with about 36 participants. Seven teams spent the week building a venture from an idea, then pitched to five judges.

IEEE's report on the workshop names Gotcha as the winning pitch. Gotcha was an exam-integrity venture that I built with Jesse Thornburg. It detected cheating in online and offline exams, and it is now GotchaExam, the first product of GotchaEducation.
