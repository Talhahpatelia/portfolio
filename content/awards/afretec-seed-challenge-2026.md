# Coach, AFRETEC SEED Challenge winners

The AFRETEC SEED Challenge is a pan-African student competition run through the AFRETEC network, which is led by Carnegie Mellon University Africa. In 2026 I coached a team with Jared Swart. The students came from Wits, Sol Plaatje University and universities in Kenya and Rwanda.

The team built Dr. Crop in 48 hours: an offline tool that diagnoses crop disease from a phone camera, using TensorFlow Lite and OpenCV, and gives farming advice in local languages. It won the agriculture track.

The photos are from the dinner at Wits, where coaches and participants received certificates of recognition and everyone came up on stage for a group photo.
