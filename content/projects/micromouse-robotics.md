# Wits MicroMouse Robotics

MicroMouse is a robotics competition in which a small autonomous robot finds its way through a maze, then races to the centre as fast as it can. Wits MicroMouse Robotics is the student team formed to compete in it. My vacation-work report on the project describes it as the first MicroMouse team in Africa, and the first project of its kind in robotics at Wits.

## My part

Dean Solomon led the group and appointed me lead engineer. I designed the first mouse and built the MVP, V1.0 and V2.0 hardware, which added Wi-Fi in V2.0. I designed the circuit boards in EasyEDA and ordered them from JLCPCB, and I helped build a modular maze for testing. Later I managed the group's administration.
