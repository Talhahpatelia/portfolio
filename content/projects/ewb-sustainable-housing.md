# Housing from recycled plastic

A low-cost housing design for migrants, built from recycled plastics and plastic bottles instead of new materials. I made it for the Global Challenge in first-year engineering at Wits, which is linked to Engineers Without Borders. Of about 1,400 students, it was ranked among the top 10 projects.

The photos show the small-scale models I built to test the idea: plastic bottles joined at orange connectors, with rods through the connectors to hold the joints.
