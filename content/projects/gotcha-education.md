# GotchaExam

GotchaExam detects cheating in online and offline exams. It was the first product of what is now GotchaEducation.

It began as Gotcha, a project I built with Jesse Thornburg. Gotcha won the pitch at the IEEE Entrepreneurship Prospectors workshop at Wits in July 2023, and in July 2024 it won R100,000 at the Allan Gray Orbis Foundation Founders Pitch.

The product's website lists online and offline proctoring, facial recognition and nose detection, student and administrator management, and secure real-time communication.
