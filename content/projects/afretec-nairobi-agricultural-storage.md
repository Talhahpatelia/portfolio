# Decentralised farm storage platform

AFRETEC is a network of African engineering universities led by Carnegie Mellon University Africa. In October 2024 I led a team at the AFRETEC Innovation Challenge at the University of Nairobi, and we took first place.

## The idea

Small farmers lose part of their harvest between the field and the market. We proposed modular storage units that sit close to farms, rather than one central warehouse, with a digital system for logistics, visibility and coordination.

## The result

First place in Nairobi. The prize included a funded two-week incubator stay in Rwanda, in January 2025. At the Wits AFRETEC event in September 2024, my team had placed second.

The University of Nairobi's [report on the 2024 Makerthon](https://afretec.uonbi.ac.ke/afretec-uon-student-makerthon-2024-a-showcase-of-innovation-and-collaboration/) covers the event, which brought together students from Nairobi and Wits.
