# Scietmeer

Scietmeer is a mobile science laboratory on six wheels, with a robotic arm. The idea was one robot that could be used for search and rescue, archaeology, architecture and farming. I designed it, 3D printed the parts, wrote the code and fitted the electrical, electronic and mechanical components.

## Results

- Gold medal, robotics, at the South African Youth Engineering and Science Symposium (SA-YESS) on 29 November 2019.
- Chosen as one of 20 projects from South Africa, Namibia, Zambia and Zimbabwe to represent South Africa at IFEST Tunisia in March 2020.
- Silver at IFEST, as one of the top 150 engineering projects worldwide.
